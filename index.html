The idea

The third space

Home is your first space. Work is your second. MAAVU is the third: a calm place in between, to slow down, reconnect and feel at ease.

The name

Maavu means mango tree in Kannada and Malayalam. Like the shade of an old mango tree, it's a place to pause, gather and stay a while.
